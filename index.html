How it goes

Bare cabinets to finished stone, in four steps.

  1. I

    Measure & template

    Once the cabinets are in: sink, cooktop, seams, overhangs.

  2. II

    Pick your slab

    Material, color, edge. See stone in person at the shop, by appointment.

  3. III

    Fabricate

    Cut to the template, cutouts made, the edge shaped and polished.

  4. IV

    Install & seal

    Set, seamed, sink mounted, sealed where it needs it, cleaned up.
